## Overview

* *Purpose:* Contains the PNG figures generated from the analysis of five cells measured using Fiji.
* **Figure 1 — area_vs_circularity.png:** Illustrates the relationship between cell area and circularity, allowing comparison of cell size and shape.
* **Figure 2 — actin_intensity.png:** Compares the mean fluorescence intensity of the five measured cells to show differences in their actin-associated fluorescence signals.
* *Data Source:* Both figures were generated using the measurements stored in data/fiji_results.csv and processed through scripts/analyze_cells.R.
* *Use in Analysis:* Provides visual representations of the measurement results to support the interpretation of cell morphology and fluorescence intensity.
